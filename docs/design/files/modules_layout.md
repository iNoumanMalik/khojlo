## The "Ahh Moment"

The secret isn't a single animation—it's **constant variety**.

If every section is a horizontal list of identical cards, the app will feel generic.

Instead, each section should have its own identity:

* **Stories** → circular avatars like Instagram.
* **Trending** → full-width editorial hero cards.
* **Nearby** → compact swipeable cards.
* **Offers** → animated coupon-style tiles.
* **Hidden Gems** → staggered masonry grid.
* **Categories** → colorful rounded chips.
* **AI Picks** → glowing glass cards with subtle motion.
* **Events** → timeline-style layout.
* **Reviews** → magazine-style photo cards.
* **Recommendations** → large immersive cards with AI explanations.

This mix of layouts keeps users visually engaged and gives the app the polished feel of products like Airbnb, Spotify, and the App Store rather than a typical CRUD application.
